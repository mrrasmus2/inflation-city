IC INFLATION CITY 2026 · V11 SOUND CITY

How to play

Goal: Finish with the highest net worth, not just the most cash. Each player starts with $5,000 and takes one turn each round.

  • Click Roll dice to see the dice animate, then watch your glowing token move clockwise one space at a time. Pass Payday to earn your current wage.
  • A constitutional election opens before the first turn and every fourth round. Choose representative democracy, public-service socialism, dictatorship, or oligarchy. Every other even round holds a policy referendum; policy votes have different weights depending on the form of government.
  • Buy unowned businesses and property, or pass. Landing on an opponent's property requires rent. Rent rises with inflation, upgrades and complete ownership of a color group.
  • Upgrade your own properties twice; sell to the bank for 70% of their current asset value. Manage up to $5,000 in new borrowing and repay debt on your turn. Outstanding debt accrues interest at the start of each new round.
  • Life Happens cards and market news change cash, CPI, wages or rates. Surprise events occur at random intervals (roughly 35–80 seconds) and may affect one player, several or the entire city. The public ledger and property registry display transactions outside the board; the city center is a decorative plaza. Only votes and major citywide news interrupt everyone. Taxes are collected every two rounds. Every active player receives an IRS summons and experiences a business disaster by Round 2.
  • Under dictatorship, one randomly chosen ruler has God Mode with unlimited executive uses, no $2,000 gameplay cap, free property transfers, unlimited tax and IRS actions, money creation and city-economy settings and choose public-good versus personal-gain executive decrees every ruler turn. Citizens periodically vote on a possible revolution; two thirds must support it. After a ruler is removed, the other players vote to confiscate and redistribute all cash, re-list their properties and impose a one-turn suspension plus permanent property-acquisition ban, or forgive the former ruler. Dictators pay no ordinary tax or IRS assessments.
  • Disasters close affected properties until any eligible student pays repair costs and becomes the new owner for no separate property purchase price. Damaged buildings continue showing fire, flood, earthquake, or power damage. Closed properties collect no rent.
  • The board stays flat while each property has a raised 3D-style building. Click a building for details. Music is optional and starts when you click Play Music. Local dice and token sounds turn on when a player interacts, and each device has its own effect controls.
  • If cash runs out, emergency borrowing happens automatically up to the credit limit. Sell assets to cover remaining shortfalls or declare bankruptcy.
  • After the selected rounds, net worth = cash + current property and upgrade values − debt. Bankrupt players are eliminated. The highest net worth wins; ties go to the earlier seat.

Classroom simulation: fictional prices, simplified economic mechanics, not actual 2026 data. The loan rate is per game round, not annual. No extra turns for doubles.
